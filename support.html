Support

Need help with QuickFocus?

Use the channels below for installation, update, license, and sync issues.

1. Support channels

  • Contact: Hugo Uchôas Borges — hugouchoas@outlook.com hugouchoas@outlook.com

2. Before opening an issue

  • Confirm you are on the latest stable installer.
  • Include steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and what happened.
  • When relevant, include app version and whether Google Drive sync is enabled.

3. Quick troubleshooting

  • Retry after restarting QuickFocus.
  • Reinstall with the latest installer if update flow fails.
  • For sync errors, disconnect/reconnect Google Drive and retry sync.
