# QuickFocus 1.8.42

## Highlights

- Added `YT Music` settings for saved YouTube links with user backup/sync persistence.
- Saved `YT Music` entries appear in the audio popup and play in loop through the official embedded player.
- Audio popup and tray controls can pause/resume or stop both native sounds and active `YT Music`.
- The `YT Music` player opens without a native title bar, supports dragging, resizing, and stays out of the Windows taskbar.
