Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

QuickFocus is designed as a local-first desktop app.

1. Local data

Tasks, settings, and productivity state are stored locally on your device.

Theme and language preferences on this website are saved in your browser localStorage.

2. Optional Google Drive sync

Google Drive sync is optional and only used when you explicitly connect and enable it.

When enabled, app data is synchronized through your Drive appDataFolder using your Google account permissions.

3. Data collection

QuickFocus does not require unnecessary personal data to run core local features.

This website does not include third-party analytics scripts at this time.

4. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Support page channels.
